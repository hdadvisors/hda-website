Subhead

HDAdvisors provides consulting services to nonprofits, housing authorities, local governments, and developers building and preserving affordable housing across Virginia.

Intro paragraph

Affordable housing work is technical and slow-moving. We help organizations move it forward, through research, financial analysis, and hands-on development experience.

Our team has spent decades on both sides of the table, as developers and as advisors. That shapes how we approach every project: we build recommendations that hold up once they meet the real constraints of financing, zoning, and staff capacity.

Services at a glance

Policy and analysis

Development advisory

Program innovation

Track record

HDA has provided affordable housing consulting since 2006. Our principals have led development corporations and policy organizations in multiple states, and that experience shapes the advice we give.

Since 2010, we've provided fractional leadership and staff to HousingForward Virginia, a statewide housing research and policy nonprofit. HDA is also a certified SWaM vendor in Virginia.
