Usage rules (from the official style guide)
  • The primary full-color logo should be the first choice on marketing materials.
  • Use the white version whenever the logo sits on a dark or photographic background.
  • Use the vertical lockup only when horizontal space is constrained.
  • Never distort the logo. Resize smaller only — never enlarge past native resolution.
  • Reduce to icon-only when the full lockup would be smaller than ~18px in type-mark height.
  • Never let text or graphic elements overlap the logo. Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "H".
