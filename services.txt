Page intro

HDAdvisors offers services across three areas: research and analysis, real estate development, and program building.

Policy and analysis

We turn housing data into decisions. Our research combines market analysis, resident input, and policy review to answer specific questions about local housing needs.

- Housing needs assessments
- Housing market research
- Data visualization and mapping
- Survey design and implementation
- Policy design and evaluation

Development advisory

We guide affordable housing developments from concept through lease-up. Our team has run this process as developers ourselves, so we plan for the financing and construction realities that show up later.

- Real estate development planning
- Due diligence and feasibility analysis
- Financial structuring and tax credit strategy, including LIHTC, historic tax credits, and new markets tax credits
- Mixed-use and mixed-income project strategy
- Community land trusts and land banks

Program innovation

We help organizations build and run housing programs, sometimes as staff. Our fractional staffing model has supported new housing organizations across Virginia since their earliest stages.

- Program design and implementation
- Program management and evaluation
- Fractional C-suite and staff support
- Fund development and strategy
- Capacity building for nonprofits and public agencies

Something not listed

We work on housing challenges beyond this list. Contact us to talk through yours. If it's not a fit for our team, we can point you to others in the field who can help.
