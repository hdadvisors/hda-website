## How we work

Our clients include nonprofits, lenders, government agencies, service providers, for-profit developers, and researchers. We help them develop, stabilize, and preserve affordable housing through feasibility studies, financial analysis, policy research, data analysis, and development consulting.

Our staff have led for-profit and nonprofit development corporations and policy organizations in several states. We have also built new construction and rehabilitation projects ourselves.

That hands-on experience shapes our research. We know federal and local housing programs in detail, we follow regional markets and policy debates closely, and we know from experience what works and what doesn't. So we give clients plans they can actually carry out.
